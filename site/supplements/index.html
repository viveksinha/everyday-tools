Sources and caveats

What the scores are
  • Review scores out of 10 from Limitless Labs. They weigh formula, dosing, label transparency and value.
  • They read the label, not the pill. Nobody here lab-tested what's inside, except for magnesium.
  • Competitor scores come from the same and similar review sites, so treat small gaps as noise.
About the prices
  • ₹ / serving is what the reviews quoted in March 2026, usually at a discount to MRP.
  • Competitor prices are rough. Some are per serving and some per pack; the table says which.
  • Check the seller before buying. Prices move every week.
Not medical advice
  • Ask a doctor first if you take medicines, are pregnant, or have kidney or liver problems.
  • Melatonin at 10 mg is a high dose; many people sleep fine on 0.5–3 mg.
Where this page differs from the source notes
  • Magnesium is marked "reviews disagree", not "best in market": the one lab test we have rated it B.
  • ZMA has no alternative listed. The notes suggested "iron supplement brands", which don't contain ZMA's ingredients.
  • Marine Collagen Citrus Burst is quoted at both ₹25.47 and ₹66.63 a serving in the source. The table uses ₹25.47.

    Glossary

    Clinical doseThe amount used in the studies that found a benefit. Less than that may do little.
    MethylcobalaminAn active form of vitamin B12. Cyanocobalamin is cheaper and has to be converted by the body first.
    K2 (MK-7)Vitamin K2. Often paired with D3 because it helps direct calcium to bone.
    KSM-66A branded ashwagandha root extract with the most human studies behind it.
    EPA + DHAThe two omega-3 fats that matter in fish oil. "1000 mg fish oil" often means only 300 mg of these.
    IFOS, Labdoor, Trustified, Informed ChoiceThird-party programmes that test supplements for purity, dose or banned substances.
    CreapureA German-made creatine monohydrate sold as a high-purity ingredient.
    ZMAZinc, magnesium and vitamin B6 in one tablet, sold for sleep and recovery.
