Why the numbers differ
  • Bigha, biswa and katha are local units with no single national value.
  • Even within a state districts and older records use different sizes.
  • For a sale or loan, go by the area in sq m or sq ft on the registered document.
Fixed units
  • Acre = 43,560 sq ft; hectare = 10,000 sq m.
  • Cent = 1/100 acre; guntha = 1/40 acre.
  • Gaj is a square yard, 9 sq ft.
Regional but fairly settled
  • Kanal = 20 marla = 5,445 sq ft in Punjab and Haryana.
  • Marla = 272.25 sq ft there; a 225 sq ft marla is also in use.
  • Ground = 2,400 sq ft, mainly in Tamil Nadu.

Which cup?
  • US customary cup is 236.6 ml; US food labels round it to 240 ml.
  • Metric cup (India, UK, Australia measuring sets) is 250 ml.
  • The gap is about 6%, enough to matter for baking.
About the gram figures
  • From King Arthur Baking's chart, which weighs a spooned-and-levelled US cup.
  • Scooped flour packs in more, so weigh it if you can.
  • Indian atta and besan can be finer than the US flours measured.
Oven settings
  • Gas mark 1 to 10 follows °F = 250 + 25 × mark.
  • The °C column is the rounded value printed on oven charts.
  • Ovens vary; the oven's own manual wins.

Fuel economy
  • km/l and mpg go up as a car gets more efficient; l/100 km goes down.
  • US and UK gallons differ: 3.785 l versus 4.546 l, so UK mpg reads about 20% higher.
Shoe sizes
  • India follows UK sizes today. An Indian system ("Bha") has been proposed but isn't in use yet.
  • US women's sizes run 2 above UK; US men's run 1 above.
  • Brands differ; foot length in cm is the safer guide.
Exact definitions
  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, both exact.
  • 1 mile = 1,609.344 m; 1 knot = 1,852 m an hour.
