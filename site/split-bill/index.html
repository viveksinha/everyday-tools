How it's worked out
  • Service charge is a % of the food subtotal.
  • GST is charged on food plus service charge, as restaurants bill it.
  • Tip is a % of the food subtotal, before tax.
By-item splits
  • A shared item is divided equally among the people ticked.
  • Service, GST and tip follow each person's share of the food.
  • An item nobody is ticked on is split across everyone.
Rounding up
  • Each share rounds up to the step you pick.
  • The extra is shown as a surplus; use it as a tip or give it back.

GST and service charge in India

Checked Nov 2026
Service charge is voluntary
  • CCPA guidelines (4 July 2022) say a restaurant can't add a service charge by default or make it mandatory.
  • The Delhi High Court upheld them on 28 March 2025; you can ask for it to be taken off the bill.
  • Restaurant groups appealed to a division bench. No final ruling found as of Nov 2026.
  • Complaints go to the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or e-Daakhil.
GST on restaurant food
  • 5% at standalone restaurants and most hotel restaurants (no input tax credit).
  • 18% at restaurants in hotels where any room is priced above ₹7,500 a night.
  • Sources disagree on exactly when the 18% applies in hotels; the bill shows the rate used.
GST on service charge
  • If you pay a service charge, GST applies to it too (CGST Act s.15, per a 2023 Lok Sabha reply).
  • A tip you add yourself after the bill isn't part of the restaurant's bill, so no GST.
