How the maths works

Earning
  • Excluded categories earn nothing and don't count toward the ₹1.5 lakh monthly threshold, so they push the accelerated slab further out of reach.
  • Each slab rounds down to whole ₹200 blocks before awarding points, the way the statement does.
  • The accelerated slab runs from ₹1.5 lakh to your credit limit + ₹1.5 lakh. Past that it drops back to the base rate.
  • Travel EDGE spend is counted separately and never contributes to the ₹1.5 lakh threshold.
Valuing the points
  • Points are worth whatever you redeem them for, so every partner carries its own ₹/point that you can edit.
  • Transfer ratios differ by partner. A 5:4 partner gives 0.8 partner points per EDGE point; the ones marked 5:2 give 0.4.
  • Group A and Group B have separate annual caps, and a few partners cap their own inbound transfers as well.
  • Anything left over is valued at the voucher rate, which is far below transfer value.

What to check yourself three things this tool can't confirm
  • Your statement beats any source here. Whether a given merchant category earned points is visible in the EDGE Rewards statement for that month.
  • Transfer ratios change. Axis reshuffled partners twice in 2026. The live ratio for your card sits in the Axis app.
  • Partner point values marked "estimate" aren't published anywhere I could verify. Replace them with your own if you redeem regularly.
